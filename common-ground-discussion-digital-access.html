Common Ground

Digital access: a discussion guide

A companion to “When digital access becomes a barrier to care.”

The finding

The reviewed evidence describes digital exclusion as well as useful telehealth adaptations. Offering a digital service does not, by itself, establish that everyone can use it.

The essential limitation

The source concerns pandemic-era evidence across refugee, immigrant, and migrant populations. It does not demonstrate the effectiveness of a specific local navigation program.

Discuss together

  1. Which step deserves closer attention in a service you know: receiving an invitation, understanding it, booking, or attending?
  2. Whose experience would be missing if you evaluated only people who successfully used the online service?
  3. What small change could you test, what outcome would you measure, and what would the result still not tell you?

Plan a next question

Complete this sentence: “We want to understand whether ______ helps ______ complete ______.”
