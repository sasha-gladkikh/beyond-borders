Common Ground

Our editorial standards

Clear writing. Sources you can check. Limits we explain.

What we publish

Common Ground publishes research commentary and perspectives on displacement, health, and access to care. We identify each contributor and the kind of work they are sharing. Research commentary interprets published studies; it does not present new research.

How we use evidence

We link to original sources and explain who was studied, when the research took place, and what it can tell us. We separate findings from the author’s interpretation and suggestions. We also distinguish a paper’s publication date from the dates of the research it draws on.

Review and disclosure

Common Ground articles are not independently peer reviewed. Any future external expert review will be identified on the relevant article, with the reviewer’s permission. Contributors should disclose relevant affiliations, funding, and potential conflicts of interest.

Perspectives and consent

Invited responses will be attributed to their contributors and published with their approval. Personal experience helps readers understand individual circumstances. We do not present one person’s account as evidence that a program works or as representative of an entire community.

Corrections and updates

When a correction changes an article’s meaning or findings, we explain it in a dated note. To report an error, send us the article title, the passage, and any information that helps us check it.
