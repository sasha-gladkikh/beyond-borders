Our editorial standards

Clear arguments. Traceable evidence. Honest limits.

What we publish

Common Ground publishes research commentary and attributed perspectives on displacement, health, and access to care. A commentary interprets published work. It is not an original study or a systematic review unless explicitly identified and supported by the appropriate methods.

How we use evidence

We link to primary sources, identify the populations and time periods studied, and explain material limitations. We distinguish reported findings from the author’s interpretation and proposed next steps. Publication dates are not substitutes for the dates of the underlying evidence.

Review and disclosure

Common Ground articles are not independently peer reviewed. Any future external expert review will be identified on the relevant article, with the reviewer’s permission. Contributors should disclose relevant affiliations, funding, and potential conflicts of interest.

Perspectives and consent

Invited responses will be attributed to their contributors and published with their approval. Personal experience offers valuable context but does not establish the effectiveness of an intervention or represent every community member’s experience.

Corrections and updates

Substantive corrections will be described in a dated note on the article. Revised articles will show their update date. To report an error, include the article title, the passage in question, and supporting information.
