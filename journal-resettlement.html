04 / Reach the appointment

How do I get there?

In our example, the next question is practical: which route, which building, and what happens if the journey takes longer than expected? A booking alone does not answer these questions.

05 / Connect the steps

Support belongs in the journey.

Clear instructions, a conversation about practical obstacles and a named contact are possible ways to support the handoff. Explore how they could fit around the same appointment.

See the same appointment with support around it.

The takeaway

Access is the whole journey.

WHO describes language, administrative and financial barriers to healthcare, and recommends practical support with registration, appointments and attendance. [1] Our argument is to examine the handoff: after a referral is offered, what remains for the person to figure out?

The animation illustrates questions a service could ask. It does not suggest every refugee encounters these obstacles, or that the proposed supports guarantee attendance. A useful next step would be to ask participants which parts of the journey were difficult and whether the support offered met their priorities.
