Common Ground

Contribute to Common Ground.

Have a question, experience, or idea about displacement and health? We would like to hear from you.

Who can contribute

Students, researchers, clinicians, community practitioners, and people with lived experience of displacement are welcome. You do not need a finished manuscript or previous publication experience to get in touch.

Choose your starting point

Your contribution might interpret research, share a community perspective, or explore a question through photography, illustration, or another medium. These are starting points, not requirements.

  • Research interpretationExplain a finding, its implications, and what remains uncertain.
  • Community perspectiveOffer insight grounded in practice or lived experience.
  • Photo essay or visual workUse images to explore displacement and health.
  • Another approachPropose the format that best serves your idea.

Send us your idea

Email a few sentences about what you would like to explore, why it matters, and how you would like to tell the story. Include your name and any sources, drafts, or examples you would like us to see.

Your idea can stand on its own or respond to something we have published. It is fine if you are still deciding on a format.

A little more guidance

Tell us about any experience or affiliation relevant to your idea and how you would like to be credited. Please do not include identifiable patient information or personal stories shared without permission.

We review proposals before publication and may suggest revisions. Sending an idea does not guarantee publication.
