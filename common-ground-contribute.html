Write for Common Ground.

Contribute your own article, research commentary, or visual essay on displacement and health.

Share an original idea

We welcome proposals from students, researchers, clinicians, community practitioners, and people with lived experience of displacement. You can pitch a new piece or respond to an existing article. Contributions are considered for publication following editorial review.

Where to begin

Choose a focused topic and tell us what readers would learn. You might examine a research paper, explain an overlooked barrier to care, share a perspective from community work, or explore a question visually. If you are responding to an existing article, include its title.

What to send

Email a working title, a short description of your idea, your name and preferred attribution, relevant affiliation or experience, and any sources you intend to use. Please do not include identifiable patient information. A proposal is an invitation to editorial discussion, not a guarantee of publication.
