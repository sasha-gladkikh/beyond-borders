Propose a response

We welcome proposals from researchers, healthcare professionals, community practitioners, and people with lived experience of displacement. Tell us which finding or question you want to address and what your perspective adds.

For our digital access commentary

Where do people encounter difficulty between receiving an invitation and attending care? What does published research help explain, and what does it miss? We invite a focused response grounded in your expertise or experience.

What to send

Email a brief proposal, your name and preferred attribution, relevant affiliation or experience, and any sources you intend to discuss. Please do not include identifiable patient information. A proposal is an invitation to editorial discussion, not a guarantee of publication.
