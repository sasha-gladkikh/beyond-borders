Contribute to Common Ground.

Bring your ideas, experiences, and creativity to the conversation on displacement and health.

Share an original idea

We welcome proposals from students, researchers, clinicians, community practitioners, and people with lived experience of displacement. Contributions can take many forms. Propose an original project, share a perspective, or build on something we have published. Ideas are considered for publication following editorial review.

Where to begin

Tell us what you want to explore, why it matters, and how you would like to present it. We are open to different formats and approaches. If your idea builds on something we have published, include a link.

What to send

Email a short description of your idea, your proposed format, your name and preferred attribution, relevant affiliation or experience, and any supporting sources or materials. Please do not include identifiable patient information. A proposal is an invitation to editorial discussion, not a guarantee of publication.
