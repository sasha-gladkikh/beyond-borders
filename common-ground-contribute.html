Common Ground

Contribute to Common Ground.

Advance the conversation on displacement and health through research, experience, and creative inquiry.

Who can contribute

We welcome proposals from students, researchers, clinicians, community practitioners, and people with lived experience of displacement. Contributions may present original work or respond to previously published content. We consider a range of formats and approaches.

Choose your starting point

Your contribution might interpret research, share a community perspective, or explore a question through photography, illustration, or another medium. These are starting points, not requirements.

  • Research interpretationExplain a finding, its implications, and what remains uncertain.
  • Community perspectiveOffer insight grounded in practice or lived experience.
  • Photo essay or visual workUse images to explore displacement and health.
  • Another approachPropose the format that best serves your idea.

Developing your proposal

Describe the topic you intend to explore, its relevance to displacement and health, and the perspective your contribution would offer. Indicate your proposed format and include a link to any published work you plan to address.

Submission requirements

Please email a brief proposal, your name and preferred attribution, any relevant affiliation or experience, and supporting sources or materials. Do not include identifiable patient information.

All proposals undergo editorial review. Submission does not guarantee publication; accepted contributions may require revisions before publication.
