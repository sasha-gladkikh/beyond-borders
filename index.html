Our Work

Three ways we advance refugee health.

  • Service

    Meet immediate needs.

    We connect people with care, essential resources, and trusted support.

  • Advocacy

    Expand access.

    We address barriers to healthcare, information, and essential services.

  • Research

    Improve what works.

    We study barriers to care and use evidence to strengthen programs and partnerships.

Why It Matters

Access should be simple, clear, and equitable.

Refugee communities often face language barriers, unfamiliar systems, and fragmented services.

Beyond Borders helps make care easier to access and navigate.
