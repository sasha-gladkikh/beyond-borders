Our approach

Three pillars. One mission.

  • Service

    Delivering direct, community-based support.

    We connect refugee families with health screenings, education, essential resources, and care navigation.

  • Advocacy

    Improving access to systems that shape health.

    We work to reduce barriers to healthcare, social services, and community resources through outreach, education, and partnership.

  • Research

    Generating evidence to strengthen refugee health.

    We study barriers to care and translate findings into more responsive programs, policies, and community interventions.

Why it matters

Access should not depend on how well someone can navigate an unfamiliar system.

Refugees often encounter fragmented services, language barriers, and limited access to trusted health information. Beyond Borders works to make care and resources more accessible, understandable, and responsive.
